Our Approach

How rankings and placements work

Listicle Liaison's network ranks vendors based on independent research, verified user reviews from G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, and other public review sources, and category-specific feature evaluation. We do offer commercial placements — Verified Profiles, Featured Placement & Editorial, and Editorial Review Subscriptions — and these are always clearly disclosed on the pages where they appear. Paid placements never override our editorial process, and our ranking criteria stay transparent and consistent.
