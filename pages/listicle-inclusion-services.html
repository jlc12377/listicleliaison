What you can buy

Three plans, one add-on, and one announcement. Nothing else is on the public price list.

1

Basic Visibility

A verified listing on the relevant city and category lists, your own content, a website or booking button, a website badge, and data updates. A business with an earned rank keeps its number. A business without one appears in a labeled "Verified listings" section, with no number. Basic never changes organic rank. Live within 2 business days of the completed onboarding form.

2

Enhanced Visibility

Everything in Basic, plus a highlighted listing labeled "Paid listing". One announcement article per quarter on SMB Newsroom, and a press release published on three news sites (newswire.com, ACCESS Newswire, and pressrelease.com). Monthly: 90-day minimum, then month to month.

3

Optimal Visibility

Everything in Enhanced, plus one clearly labeled "Sponsored" spot above the organic list in that category. There is one per category. It sits outside the numbered list and is never a rank. The announcement article and press release go out monthly. Monthly: 90-day minimum, then month to month.

Announcements and tracking

01

One-time Announcement

For businesses that don't want a monthly plan. One announcement article on SMB Newsroom, a press release on the three news sites, and 3 months of Basic Visibility. After that, renew Basic or let it lapse. The free entry, and any earned rank, stays either way.

02

AI Visibility Tracking

+$49/mo on any plan. A monthly report from ai-mentions.com showing whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude mention the business. It covers one business. It is never included in a plan. Without a plan, standalone tracking is $65/mo at ai-mentions.com.

03

Monthly report

Every client gets a monthly report email with links to where you're published, listing views and clicks, and (with tracking) your AI score trend. No login needed.
